# YouTube Publishing Bible — Storybook Stickman Studio (All Niches)

Dokumen standar untuk meracik paket publikasi YouTube lengkap (**Judul, Deskripsi, Tags, Blueprint Thumbnail, dan Pengaturan Upload**) di seluruh genre stickman:
1. *Extreme Survival Physiology*
2. *Extreme Industrial Protocols & Catastrophe SOPs*
3. *Global Strategic Chokepoints & Hidden Monopolies*
4. *Illustrated Ancient History & Daily Life*

Tujuan utama: Memaksimalkan **CTR (Click-Through Rate > 8-12%)**, **AVD (Average View Duration > 50-65%)**, dan **RPM Monetisasi Iklan (Tier 1 US/UK/DE/CA)**.

---

## 1. Pengaturan Upload YouTube Studio (Monetization & Algorithm Setup)

| Parameter | Konfigurasi Standar | Alasan Algoritmik |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | `Education` | Disukai pengiklan korporat (iklan B2B, software, asuransi, teknologi finansial, edukasi) dengan nilai RPM tertinggi ($8–$25+ per 1.000 tayang di negara Tier 1). |
| **Type (Education)** | `None` *(atau Concept overview)* | Opsi `Problems` hanya untuk tutorial soal ujian sekolah. Mengosongkan `Type` menjaga video masuk ke rekomendasi audiens umum YouTube. |
| **Language & Captions** | `English (United States)` | Target utama penonton global bernilai RPM tinggi. |
| **License** | `Standard YouTube License` | Mencegah konten di-reupload tanpa izin. |
| **Allow Embedding** | `Yes` | Membantu distribusi di forum Reddit, Hacker News, dan blog eksternal. |

## 5. Strategi Tags & Keywords

Gunakan kombinasi 15–20 tag berjenjang:
1. **Tier 1 (Specific Long-Tail):** Persis kata kunci judul dan subjek inti (`hypothermia paradox`, `nuclear coolant failure protocol`, `asml lithography monopoly`, `roman urine tax`).
2. **Tier 2 (Niche Broad):** Topik industri/sains (`survival physiology`, `forensic engineering`, `catastrophe protocols`, `global supply chain`, `ancient roman daily life`).
3. **Tier 3 (Channel / Format):** Format konten (`animated documentary`, `stickman animation`, `educational video essay`, `how it works`).
