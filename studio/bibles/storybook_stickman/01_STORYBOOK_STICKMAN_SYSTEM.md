## 2. Strategic Breakdown: 3 Core Niches

### Niche 1: Extreme Survival Physiology (Fisiologi Bertahan Hidup Ekstrem)
* **Core Thesis:** Jam demi jam keruntuhan biokimia dan organ tubuh manusia saat dihadapkan pada batas fisik absolut. Mekanisme kompensasi tubuh yang awalnya melindungi justru berbalik menjadi bumerang mematikan.
* **Audience Psychology:** *Morbid curiosity* universal. Rasa takut fisik mendasar (kedinginan, kehabisan udara, dehidrasi) dikemas ilmiah tanpa visual sadis (*gore*).
* **The Hour-by-Hour Collapse Flow:**
  1. *The Trigger (0:00–1:00):* Tubuh kehilangan kestabilan (suhu ambruk, dehidrasi, lonjakan tekanan barometrik).
  2. *The Compensation Phase (1:00–4:00):* Organ vital mengorbankan perifer (vasokonstriksi ekstrem, penutupan sirkulasi ujung jari).
  3. *The Biological Trap (4:00–8:00):* Malfungsi kompensasi fatal (halusinasi nitrogen, paradoxical undressing, asidosis darah).
  4. *The Final Verdict (Penutup):* Batas absolut seluler dan post-mortem deductions sains modern.
* **High-CTR Topics:**
  1. *Why Freezing to Death Makes You Feel Burning Hot (The Hypothermia Paradox)*
  2. *The Nitrogen Rapture: What 100 Meters of Ocean Depth Does to the Human Brain*
  3. *Hour 72 Without Water: The Exact Sequence of Cellular Dehydration*
  4. *The Death Zone: What Breathing at 8,000 Meters Does to Red Blood Cells*
  5. *The CO2 Trap: Why Cave Spelunkers Suffocate Without Ever Feeling Out of Breath*

### Niche 2: Extreme Industrial Protocols & Catastrophe SOPs (Protokol Kegagalan Industri)

  4. *The Industrial Legacy (Penutup):* Regulasi keselamatan baru yang lahir dari tragedi tersebut.
* **High-CTR Topics:**
  1. *The 60-Second Protocol When a Nuclear Reactor Loses All Primary Coolant*
  2. *Saturation Diving: The Exact 28-Day Procedure Inside a Pressurized Steel Chamber*
  3. *What Submarine Crews Are Ordered to Do When the Pressure Hull Breaches at 200m*
  4. *Blowout Preventer Failure: The 3 Minutes Before an Offshore Oil Rig Explodes*
  5. *The Runway Incursion Protocol: How Air Traffic Controllers Handle a Fog Blindspot*

## 6. Voiceover & Audio Engineering Specifications

* **Voice Character:** Bariton dokumenter investigatif (dewasa, berwibawa, tempo tenang, sentuhan *dry deadpan wit*).
* **ElevenLabs Settings:**
  * Voice Preset: **"George"**, **"Marcus"**, atau **"Adam"**
  * Stability: **50% – 55%**
  * Clarity / Similarity: **75% – 80%**
  * Style Exaggeration: **10% – 15%**
* **Script Cadence:**
  * **Pacing:** 135 – 145 WPM.
  * **Engineering Pause:** Sisipkan tag `[pause 0.8s]` setelah angka penting, paradoks, atau konsekuensi fatal.
  * **Sentence Length:** Maksimal 15 kata per kalimat untuk menjaga ritme tetap renyah.

## 7. Editing, Pacing, & Sound Design

| Parameter | Standar Operasional |
|---|---|
| **Durasi Visual** | 5 hingga 7 detik per visual frame (mencegah *visual fatigue*). |
| **Camera Movement** | *Slow Zoom-in* halus (100% ke 105%) atau *Slow Pan* horizontal memakai kurva *Ease-In-Out*. |
| **Punch-in Cut** | *Snap zoom* instan (115%) ke ekspresi wajah stickman saat ada anomali atau krisis genting. |
| **Foley Layer 1 (Ambience)** | Deru angin badai salju, desis pipa tekanan tinggi, dengung generator pelabuhan (-24 dB). |
| **Foley Layer 2 (Action)** | Langkah salju *crunch*, tuas ditarik *heavy clank*, tombol ditekan *click*, gemeretak es (-14 dB). |
| **Backsound (BGM)** | *Dark Ambient Investigative Synth Pulses* pada volume **-18 dB** di bawah suara narasi. |
